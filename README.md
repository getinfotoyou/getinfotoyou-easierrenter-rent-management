## Frequently Asked Questions About Rental Property Management

### How does EasierRenter help me monitor late rent payments?
EasierRenter maintains an active calendar of due dates for every occupied unit in your portfolio. If a payment is not logged by the due date, the app highlights the past-due balance on your dashboard. This prompt reminder helps you reach out to tenants right away, refer to your agreed lease terms, and prevent unpaid rent from accumulating into a larger problem.

### Can I track multi-unit buildings alongside single-family homes?
Yes, the app accommodates both multi-family configurations and standalone rental houses. You can create a parent property for a duplex, triplex, or apartment building and nest individual units underneath it, each with its own lease, rent amount, and tenant history. Single-family properties can be managed as individual units with the same detailed tracking.

### Is my tenant and rental financial data kept private?
EasierRenter values user privacy and stores your property records directly on your Android device. Your tenant contact lists, payment histories, and rental income data remain under your direct control, rather than being mined or shared by third-party data brokers.

### How does the app assist with annual tax preparation for landlords?
By recording every repair bill, utility payment, and contractor expense against specific rental units throughout the year, EasierRenter creates an organized record of your deductible operating expenses. When tax season arrives, you can reference your cumulative annual totals instead of sorting through shoe boxes of faded paper receipts.
